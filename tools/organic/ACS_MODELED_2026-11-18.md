# ACS-modeled batch (2026-11-18): src/28A-zz2-ch01–ch10.json, 28B-zz2-ch11–ch16.json

321 original questions (28A +200, 28B +121), modeled on two sources:
- **Primary:** the topic blueprint of the ACS Exams *Official Guide* for organic chemistry, chapter by chapter (its "specific topics covered" lists, question formats and difficulty).
- **Secondary:** the Reusch problem sets (organicchemistrydata.org).

Neither source is reproduced. Both are copyrighted: the Official Guide is a commercial ACS Exams book, and Reusch is "All rights reserved". Writers recorded a fingerprint (molecule + ask) for every source item they read and reused none. The fingerprint lists stayed outside the repository.

**Process**
1. One writer per two guide chapters:
   - blueprint from the chapter;
   - coverage count against our bank;
   - about 20 questions per chapter aimed at the gaps.
2. Independent originality audit of all 321 against the guide page images and the Reusch text. It flagged 9 items as too close (1 clear, 8 near). All 9 were rewritten with new molecules and a new ask, then re-audited clean.
3. Blind solve of all 321, plus the 15 rewritten or replaced items again: 0 key errors.
4. Wording clarified where an ACS-level student could defend a second answer: stated rearrangements, KOtBu instead of ethoxide, and a replaced β,γ-enone distractor.
5. Validator: 0 errors, 0 warnings.
6. Click-through at 1440, 375 and 320 px.
