**Play:** https://itsdrdan.github.io/finalexam/

## Links that open exactly what you want

Start with your course link, then add any of these, each starting with `&`:

| Add | What it does | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `&mode=topic&topic=KEY` | Topic Training on one topic (keys below) | `?course=1A&mode=topic&topic=gases` |
| `&topic=KEY1,KEY2` | Several topics mixed, for a chapter that spans two | `?course=3A&topic=measure,matter` |
| `&mode=boss` | Opens the Boss Battle (timed practice final) | `?course=1B&mode=boss` |
| `&exam=mid` or `&exam=final` | CHEM 3B only: Organic Midterm or Biochem Final Boss Battle | `?course=3B&exam=final` |
| `&len=half` | Half Battle (half the questions and time) | `?course=1A&len=half` |
| `&style=examday` | Exam Day style: directions and a bubble sheet, only the reference sheets the real exam gives, no Boss HP, pace meter or topic labels | `?course=28A&style=examday` |
| `&mode=weak`, `&mode=quick`, `&mode=blitz` | Weak Spots, Quick 10, or Blitz | `?course=28B&mode=quick` |

`exam`, `len` and `style` open the Boss Battle on their own and can be combined, for example a half-length Organic Midterm in Exam Day style:
https://itsdrdan.github.io/finalexam/?course=3B&exam=mid&len=half&style=examday

They only choose the starting buttons; students can still change them. A student who already has a Boss Battle in progress for that course and exam picks up where they left off.

## What's in it

- 5,556 original multiple-choice questions written in the style of standardized chemistry finals. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any exam publisher.
- Works on phones over slow connections: only the selected course downloads, and courses a student has opened work offline afterward.
- Every question has a hint and a step-by-step explanation, and every wrong answer explains why it's wrong.
- Modes: Boss Battle (timed practice final, Practice or Exam Day style), Topic Training, Weak Spots, Quick 10, Blitz, and a Daily 5.
- The CHEM 1A and 1B Boss Battles (70 questions, 110 minutes) weight topics by the chapter outline of the ACS Exams Institute's Official Guide for General Chemistry (first term: Toolbox and chapters 1–8; second term: chapters 9–16). The guide gives no percentages, so each chapter counts in proportion to its study and practice questions. CHEM 1B Colligative Properties is practice-only and never appears in a Boss Battle.
- The CHEM 28A and 28B Boss Battles weight topics by the chapter outline of the ACS Exams Institute's Official Guide for Organic Chemistry (28A: first-term chapters 1–10; 28B: all chapters 1–16, like the ACS full-year exam, with first-term chapters drawn from the 28A bank), in proportion to each chapter's length. Topics follow the Fresno City College course outlines.
- Every Boss Battle has the same number of questions per topic (set by the weights above) **and the same mix of difficulty levels**, so two students' practice finals are equally hard. Near-identical questions (same molecule or figure, same idea) never appear together in one Boss Battle.
- Each question was checked one by one in the November 2026 audit (answer key, every wrong choice, explanation, hint, figure, course fit); the item-level record is in `tools/audit-2026-11-18-acs/`. This is independent practice, not an ACS product: it is not affiliated with, endorsed by or psychometrically equated to any ACS exam.
- Built-in periodic table, data sheet and a TI-36X Pro style practice calculator.

## Using it in Canvas

**As a link:** in a module, choose Add item → External URL, paste your course link, and tick "Load in a new tab".

**Embedded in a page:** edit a Canvas page, switch to the HTML editor, and paste:

```html
<iframe src="https://itsdrdan.github.io/finalexam/?course=1A" title="Final Boss! Final Exam Review" width="100%" height="900" style="border:0" allow="fullscreen"></iframe>
```

Change `course=1A` to your course (and add any options from the table above). The game resizes the frame to fit, so students don't get a scrollbar inside a scrollbar.

Good to know:

- Progress (XP, Weak Spots, saved Boss Battles) is kept in the student's own browser. Some browsers, Safari especially, keep an embedded copy's progress separate from the same page opened directly, so tell students to use the same link each time.
- Nothing is graded and no scores come back to Canvas. If you want credit for practice, ask students to upload a screenshot of their Boss Battle results page.
- Add `&prof=YourLastName` to a link (for example `&prof=Gilley`) to put your name in the game's "Rate this game" list, so student feedback from your class is labeled with your name.

## Reporting a problem with a question

Every explanation ends with the question's ID (for example **Question 1A-198**) and a **⚑ Report a problem** box. Students and instructors pick what looks wrong (answer key, unclear wording, two right answers, typo or figure) and can add a note. Reports go straight to Dr. Dan with the question ID; no name or other personal information is sent. The ID is also handy for pointing students to one question ("look at 1A-198").

## Privacy

No logins and no names. Progress stays in the student's own browser. Practice stats are anonymous: which question, which choice, seconds spent on it, and, for Boss Battles, a random code made for that one exam so its answers can be analyzed together (which questions separate strong from weak performance, and how long the exam really takes). Students can turn stats off with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Problem reports send only the question ID, the reason picked and the optional note.
